The two branches never see each other: owner-calls finds the pain, new-jobs-test proves the
fixed agent. The audit is where they meet. Both must answer these the same way:

1. **Which pain is the audit testing?** It must be the pain 6 or more of 15 owners picked in
   `owner-calls.md`, and it must be a pain the agent was tested on in run 4.
2. **What does "ready" mean for a job?** One definition, from `the-tool.md`, used by both the
   run-4 answer key and the audit's comparison with the person's packet.
3. **Which rung of `office/_record/ladder.md` does the agent work at in the audit?** No higher
   than the rung run 4 passed at.
4. **Is the audited business one of the owners called?** Say yes or no and why, so the audit
   is not read as proof of a pain that business never named.
5. **What counts as a pass?** The numbers from `done-for-one-fix.md` (the to-set-numbers
   decision), not a number chosen after the results are in.
